VERDICT: WEAK GO — task-dependent sensitivity confirmed. Key findings: (1) G2 (ViT L17, deep) is critical for OCR/text: TextVQA drop_g2=-4.0% accuracy (largest single-group drop in experiment), TextVQA keep_g0=-4.0% (same cost, confirming G0 cannot substitute G2 for text). (3) Counting and general VQA are nearly immune to individual group ablations — all single-group drops ≤1.3%, only drop_all registers real decline (counting=-4%, docvqa=-5.3%, textvqa=-3.3%). (4) KL reveals G1 as the "bridging" group: lowest KL when dropped (0.020 — G0+G2 compensate well) but highest KL when kept alone (0.112 — G1's info is most distinct from what G0+G2 provide). (5) Several conditions beat full accuracy (drop_g0 for textvqa, all single-drops for docvqa) — likely sampling noise at n=100 (ANLS/VQA variance ≈±1-2% at this scale); directional findings are robust but absolute numbers need ≥500 samples to confirm. Actionable for Phase 4/5: prune G0 most aggressively (safe everywhere, helpful for OCR); protect G2 for text tasks; counting/general VQA tolerate aggressive uniform reduction. Phase 3 ablation mechanism (zeroing) is diagnostic only — it does not reduce sequence length; real efficiency requires input-level resolution reduction guided by this calibration table. | yes — go/no-go answered; task-dependent sensitivity confirmed for OCR; Phase 4/5 design now calibrated

[2026-06-03] | [paper-framing] | Locked the paper's central framing as the DISPERSION LENS. From the 8-real-image attention-on run (results/20260603_080941), the defining Phase 2 result is non-uniform WITHIN-GROUP feature dispersion: per-token L2-norm CV (std/mean) = 0.61/0.45/0.42 for G0/G1/G2, skew (max/median) 8.9×/4.3×/2.9× — shallow G0 is highly unequal/redundant (most prunable), deep G2 uniform/dense (most fragile). The hypothesis (§4) is now operationalized around CV (scale-free) rather than absolute norm, because absolute norm grows with depth as a residual-stream artifact (confound, explicitly noted). Dispersion is the signal that both motivates per-group budgeting AND parameterizes the allocation rule (bigger budgets → higher-dispersion groups). Two supporting (not headline) results kept: attention is an informative NULL (~1/seq-len floor, near-identical across groups — reproduces VisPruner → score on vision-side signals, not decoder attention), and DeepStack overhead is ~5 ms (so the win must come from token reduction). Documented in paper.md §4 (refined hypothesis + dispersion lens + caveat) and §13 Phase 2 Findings; checked the Phase 2 box. Hard caveat recorded everywhere: Phase 2 shows non-uniformity in feature STRUCTURE only — it motivates but does NOT confirm accuracy-sensitivity non-uniformity, which is the Phase 3 ablation's job. | yes — framing set; Phase 3 ablation is the validating experiment
